what is your vehicle doing?

needle + nozzle

Black smoke is unburnt fuel: a worn needle seat lets fuel drip, and the nozzle delivers it as droplets instead of a mist.

control valve

If the valve lets too much fuel flow back, pressure cannot build at start-up and the engine is slow to start.

nozzle + valve

Clogged nozzle holes or a leaking valve cannot deliver the fuel needed when you press the pedal.

nozzle + needle

A nozzle that does not spray evenly cannot atomise fuel well, so consumption rises. Cleaning and adjustment often bring it close to factory values.

whole set

Even without symptoms, injectors wear over time. On the bench, all four injectors are measured and adjusted together.
